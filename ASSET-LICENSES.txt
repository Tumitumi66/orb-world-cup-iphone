V6 audio credits / sources

Gunshot Sounds — Tabasco — CC0
https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
Recorded CZ-52, Mosin Nagant, SKS and shotgun samples. Trimmed, normalized and edited for pistol, automatic, sniper, shotgun and low pitched blast cues. Gatling/rocket cues are designed edits, not dedicated recordings of those weapons.

20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes) — StarNinjas — CC0
https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
Sword attacks and metal shield clashes.

Swishes Sound Pack — CC0
https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
Physical object swishes for knife and spear.

ImpactPlate_medium_000 — Kenney Impact Sounds — CC0
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Mirror used: https://github.com/manuel-palacio/brickstorm/blob/main/CREDITS.md
https://github.com/manuel-palacio/brickstorm/blob/main/public/audio/paddle-hit.ogg

Ice/magic and UI cues retain original designed effects. All samples are normalized before playback; master gain and limiter cap mixed playback.
